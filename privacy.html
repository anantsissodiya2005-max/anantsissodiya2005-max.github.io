CreatorOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

CreatorOS is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels (SimplyTells and MyPOV). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, CreatorOS uses YouTube API Services to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own channels, to confirm which channel is signed in, and to read public statistics of the owner's own videos.

What it stores

Public statistics of the owner's videos (titles, views, likes, comments, dates, durations), kept in the owner's private storage for analysis. Sign-in tokens are stored only in the owner's computer keychain. No data about other people is collected, shared or sold.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can delete the stored statistics at any time and can revoke CreatorOS's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

CreatorOS uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
