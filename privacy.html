CreatorOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

CreatorOS is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels (SimplyTells and MyPOV). It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

CreatorOS uses YouTube API Services. By using CreatorOS you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in (OAuth), CreatorOS uses YouTube API Services to: upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own channels (with a scheduled publish time); confirm which channel is signed in; and read public statistics of the owner's own videos (titles, views, likes, comments, publish dates, durations).

How it is used, stored and shared

The statistics are used only for the owner's own analysis and are kept in the owner's private storage, refreshed daily. Public counts (views, likes, comments) and CreatorOS's own calculations from them, which are labelled as CreatorOS's and not YouTube's, are kept for up to 36 months. Other YouTube data, such as titles and descriptions, is refreshed daily and deleted after 30 days. Sign-in tokens are stored only in the keychain on the owner's computer. No data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to anyone. No data about other people is collected.

Cookies and device storage

CreatorOS does not use cookies or tracking. The only information stored on a device is the owner's sign-in token, in the computer's keychain.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke CreatorOS's access at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked or the owner asks, the stored sign-in token and statistics are deleted within 7 days. CreatorOS never deletes or edits videos on YouTube.
